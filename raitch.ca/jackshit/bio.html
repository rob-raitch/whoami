Jack Shit - songwriter, lyricist, guitarist, vocalist.
He is no man and yet every man at once.
A play on words with the release of Jack's solo efforts Jacks Hit - you can all be in on the joke.
People don't know Jack Shit but if they spend the time, they can learn a little bit with each moment...

Jack is working on acoustic guitar material and an album will be forthcoming in the year 2003 some time. There will be lots of friends and special guests making appearances as Jack wants to share his love of music creation with the world.

Jack has been involved with bands since the late 1980s with efforts that started with Iron Maiden & Metallica cover songs through his current role as a five-string bass fill-in with a fledgling project named Permanent Damage. Through this era, it has been a mish-mash of the heavy-edged styles of rock, metal, punk and hardcore. The band names of Speakeasy, Solosex & Anything You Want were used for specific line-ups throughout the 1990s.

With Jacks Hit, Jack has evolved to folksy, emotive songwriting, much less mathematical and cerebral than previous efforts.

With the release of these two cover songs available on the website, Jack has attempted to juxtapose the angry styles of DRI & Discharge with the essence of a good raw folk treatment. Anyone who grew up with this music knows it is fundamentally folk music for them by them.
